## Hard Company-Readiness Gates

- [ ] **Demand:** 5 independent design partners
- [ ] **Live traffic:** ≥ 3 running live traffic
- [ ] **Usage:** ≥ 1,000,000 real production decisions observed
- [ ] **Retention:** ≥ 3 users keep Microloop enabled for ≥ 4 weeks and indicate they would care if removed
- [ ] **Value:** ≥ 2 partners demonstrate meaningful whole-system impact (≥ 5% whole-app call reduction OR meaningful latency improvement OR customer-defined KPI)
- [ ] **Safety:** Real-world verified error and revocation behavior measured and publicly scoped
- [ ] **Commercial:** ≥ 1 paid pilot OR ≥ 2 written commitments to pay after agreed pilot criteria

## YC-Ready Gate

- [ ] 5–10 independent teams
- [ ] 3–5 retained production users
- [ ] Millions of real decisions
- [ ] Clear whole-app denominator
- [ ] Measured economics
- [ ] At least one paid customer
- [ ] One exceptional case study

Target sentence (only use when numbers are real):

> Three companies have had Microloop running continuously for over a month. Across X million production decisions, it eliminated Y% of total application model calls / Z hours of latency, while automatically revoking N stale fast paths.

---

## Failure Conditions

Change direction if:

- < 10% of contacted ICPs have relevant bounded/verifiable traffic
- Teams consistently say 3–10% whole-system savings is irrelevant
- Integration/trust burden outweighs economic value
- Cheap-model routing solves the same pain well enough
- Independent outcome verification is too difficult in most real systems
- Users disable Microloop after pilots
